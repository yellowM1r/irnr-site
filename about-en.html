What is IRNR?

This website is designed as an official government portal, but it is a fictional role-play and humor project.

It's a joke

The Israeli Rostov People's Republic is not a real existing state. Government bodies, offices, laws, currency, diplomatic relations, news and other elements on this website belong to a fictional setting.

All characters, offices, appointments, events, allegations and other circumstances described here are part of the fictional role-play.

References to real names or the use of public figures' photographs do not mean that those people actually hold the stated offices, have any relationship with IRNR, or carried out the described actions.

In particular, court materials and allegations published on this portal are fictional story elements and should not be understood as reports of real court cases.
